1. Overview

29 Card Game (“the Game”) is the Bangladeshi trick-taking card game Twenty-Nine, played against computer players or with friends on the same Wi-Fi network. It is developed by sadid.dev (“we”). This policy explains what information the Game handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: the Game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your game data stays on your device. The only data that leaves your device for a company is what Google’s advertising service, AdMob, collects to show ads in the Android version.

3. Information stored on your device

The Game saves one file in its private app storage on your device. It contains:

  • your settings (rules, bot difficulty, table theme, speed, sound, language);
  • your statistics (matches and deals played and won, contracts, Pairs, single hands);
  • the match in progress, so you can resume it;
  • the player name you typed, if you typed one (you can skip it);
  • the time until which you chose to play without ads, if you watched a rewarded ad.

This file is never sent to us or to any server by the Game. You can reset your statistics in Stats → Reset stats, change your name from the Name chip on the title screen, and delete everything by uninstalling the Game or clearing its data in Android Settings. If you have turned on Android’s own device backup, Android may include this file in the backup to your Google account; that backup is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

4. Wi-Fi multiplayer (“Play with friends”)

When you choose Play with friends, the Game connects to other devices on the same local network (Wi-Fi or a phone’s hotspot). It does not use the internet for this and it does not go through any server of ours.

  • A hosting device announces its game on the local network (its player name, the open seats and its local IP address), so nearby devices can find it.
  • While you play, your player name and your moves are sent to the other players’ devices in that match, and theirs to yours. Each player sees only their own cards.
  • Nothing from the match is kept after it ends, except your own statistics on your own device.
  • There is no chat. The only text you share is your player name, which you choose (up to 16 characters). Do not use a name you want to keep private.

5. Advertising (Android version)

The Android version is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banners on the title screen, the pause menu and the match-end screen; occasional full-screen ads between deals (never during a hand); and an optional rewarded video that gives you 30 minutes without ads. The Game never shows ads in a tutorial lesson or a Wi-Fi match.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and sends to Google:

  • device identifiers: your device’s advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, from which a general (approximate) location may be estimated;
  • app and device information, and your interactions with ads (views, taps, video plays);
  • diagnostic information such as ad load times and the SDK’s crash reports.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports in AdMob. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland (and where else the law requires it), the Game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before any ad is requested, and personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy options on the title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can play without any ads for 30 minutes at a time by watching a rewarded video, and the Game always works offline, where no ads are loaded.

The Windows version (Microsoft Store) shows no ads and contains no advertising code.

7. What we do not collect

The Game does not ask for or collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location, and it has no accounts. We do not sell your information.

9. Data retention and deletion

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google’s policies; you can manage it with your advertising ID settings and your Google account. If you have a question, write to sadid.jones@gmail.com.

10. Children’s privacy

The Game is a traditional card game for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help.

11. Security

The Game’s save file is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Wi-Fi multiplayer traffic stays on your local network and is not encrypted, so play on networks you trust. No system is completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects what data is collected, we will update this page before that version of the Game is released.
